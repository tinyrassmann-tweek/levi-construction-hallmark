BEFORE WE BUILD

A few good questions.

What happens after I request a consultation?

Your request is saved for the Levi team. We’ll use the contact information you provide to discuss the property, the work you have in mind, and whether a site visit is the right next step. A request does not create a construction contract.

Can I see changes to cost or timing before approving them?

Yes. The portal includes change requests with their scope, price adjustment, and schedule impact. Your recorded decision stays with the project.

How do I raise a project concern?

Use the portal’s Concerns tab to describe the issue. The team can assign an owner, record the next action, and set a response date. You can review a proposed resolution and confirm or reopen the concern.

Are the homes pictured completed Levi projects?

The new home and kitchen images on this page are AI-generated design inspiration, not photographs of completed Levi projects. Ask the team about relevant project examples during your consultation.
